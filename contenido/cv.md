## Profile

<p class="lede">My research studies the subjective perception of multimedia content: what we remember from
a video, how an advertisement persuades us, and what impression a person leaves when we
watch them speak. I model these phenomena by adapting transformers and multimodal language
models with parameter-efficient techniques, and I contrast them against biological
signals —electroencephalography in particular— to bring prediction closer to mechanism.</p>
