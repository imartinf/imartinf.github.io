Project 01 · BSc / MSc

Modelling persuasion strategies in advertising

Automatic systems that encourage a more responsible consumption of advertising.

Three modalities enter the model together; the persuasion strategies come out.

  • Survey the state of the art in machine learning and neuromarketing.
  • Implement models that predict the persuasion strategies of an ad, in Python (PyTorch, Huggingface).
  • Evaluate the proposed solutions with objective metrics.
IMFBSc and MSc theses · 2026-2703

Project 02 · BSc / MSc

Predicting memorability from brain signals

Can we tell whether someone remembered a video from their EEG?

The EEG recorded during viewing feeds a model that predicts whether the video will be remembered.

  • Survey the previous literature on predicting memorability from brain signals.
  • Analyse and preprocess the EEG data.
  • Implement models that predict video memorability, evaluated with objective metrics.
IMFBSc and MSc theses · 2026-2704

Project 03 · BSc / MSc

Persuasive design in digital gambling

Risks to financial and social wellbeing when play stops being responsible.

Interface elements are analysed to flag the risk factors they carry.

  • Survey the previous literature on multimodal analysis of digital gambling.
  • Collect and preprocess the relevant data sources.
  • Implement models that detect persuasion, intermittent rewards and misleading promotions, evaluated with objective metrics.
